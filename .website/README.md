# Release the Rainbow
The rainbow is trapped in a crystal prism.  It's a rainbow prison! Navigate the maze and zoom in on walls to reveal the trapped triangle shards. Fill all the color cubes to make the rainbow whole again.

## Goal
Collect every rainbow triangle and complete all six color cubes.

## Controls
- Turn left/right/up/down (arrow keys, or tap the screen edges)
- Move forward (space, or tap the center)
- Powerups (letter key or tap the square)

You can turn 90 degrees or move forward, not both at once. Move forward while facing a wall to reveal any triangles, then tap the triangle to collect.  Four triangles make a cube face, six faces complete a color cube. Each maze level is generated and they get larger as you go.

## Unicorn Powerups
- **W - Wash:** briefly colors walls containing triangle shards
- **E - Extract:** pulls triangles of one color straight into your collection
- **R - Rally:** adds completed faces directly to your color cubes

Upgrades make powerups stronger and more frequent. Each has a cooldown, but you can use them as often as you like: no cost, no penalty, use them liberally.

Total time is recorded so you can try to beat your best.
